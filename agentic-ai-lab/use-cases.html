USE CASE 01 · ACCOUNTS PAYABLEINVOICE CONTROL TEST

Duplicate invoice and payment-risk screening

Business question: are the same supplier, amount, purchase order and invoice attributes repeated, and is there evidence that both records were actually paid?

RecordSupplierInvoiceAmountPOPaid?
DECISION LOGICEXPLAINABLE MATCH
1. Normalise supplier, invoice and PO identifiers.
2. Candidate match when supplier + amount + PO align and invoice IDs are exact or near matches.
3. Check payment ledger independently.
4. If payment evidence is missing, classify as a potential duplicate invoice—not duplicate payment.
5. Route high-risk candidates to AP owner and audit reviewer.

LaneVolumeRevenue €mCost €mDiscountMargin

AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES & TOOL BOUNDARIESROLE DESIGN

Orchestrator

Plans tasks, selects specialist workflow, enforces scope and waits for required dependencies. Cannot approve its own findings.

Data Intelligence

Profiles data, validates keys and executes read-only SQL/Python checks. Cannot alter source records.

Domain specialists

Finance, procurement, pricing, fleet and risk agents run only their approved control catalogue.

Audit Challenger

Tests evidence sufficiency, alternative explanations and unsupported causal or legal claims.

Reporting agent

Creates structured workpapers with evidence IDs, test steps, result, limitation and owner action.

Human control owner

Approves scope, adjudicates findings, authorises external actions and closes remediation.

TOOL / PLATFORM MAPPINGPROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION
Data & query: BigQuery or governed warehouse, SQL, dbt-style transformations and data contracts.

Analytics: Python, pandas, SciPy / scikit-learn for profiling, statistics and explainable anomaly scoring.

Agent runtime: Gemini or another approved LLM through a controlled framework such as LangGraph; tool schemas, bounded context and structured outputs.

Enterprise platform: Palantir Foundry could host governed data products / workflows where licensed and available; this demo does not use Foundry.

BI: Power BI or Qlik Sense for KPI and control dashboards. Demo visuals are custom HTML, not Power BI reports.

Governance: OIDC / SSO, RBAC, secrets manager, encryption, immutable audit trail, model/prompt registry, monitoring and approval workflow.
HOW THE SYSTEM MAKES A DECISIONEXPLAINABLE RISK ROUTING
A · SIGNAL

Rule / model output

Every signal must identify the rule or model version, source records, run ID, threshold and calculation. A model score alone is never a finding.

B · VALIDATION

Evidence + challenge

Cross-check the source, test missing evidence, compare alternative explanations and separate correlation from causation. Downgrade or suppress unsupported claims.

C · ROUTING

Risk tier + human gate

Prioritise by impact, likelihood, evidence strength and urgency. High-risk or regulated outcomes require an accountable human reviewer before action.

GOVERNANCE, GDPR & MODEL LIFECYCLENON-NEGOTIABLE CONTROLS

Before deployment

Use-case inventory and risk classification; lawful purpose and data minimisation; DPIA where required; access design; source approvals; model validation; red-team and security review.

At runtime

Least privilege, read-only by default, secrets isolation, prompt-injection defence, PII filtering, provenance, trace IDs, human approval, retention limits and incident escalation.

After deployment

Monitor quality, drift, hallucination, bias, latency and cost; review feedback and overrides; version prompts/models/tools; revalidate material changes; rollback and decommission safely.
