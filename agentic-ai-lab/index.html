02 / OPERATING WORKFLOW

From issue identification to verified disposition

The implemented operating principle connected automated issue flagging with accountable specialist review. The sequence below describes the workflow pattern, not a claim that every step was autonomously executed by an AI model.

PROCESS MAPPING
IMPLEMENTED WORKFLOW · ISSUE IDENTIFICATION

AI-assisted monitoring and triage

  1. Define the business process and relevant control context
  2. Review available operational data and supporting evidence
  3. Apply configured checks and AI-assisted analysis
  4. Surface potential issues, missing information or unusual patterns
  5. Attach context and route exceptions to the relevant specialist
  6. Track verification and human disposition before closure
Operational value: earlier visibility of potential issues, structured hand-off and a clearer path to specialist verification.
→
ACCOUNTABILITY · SPECIALIST VERIFICATION

Human-led validation and decision

  1. Review the flagged issue and source references
  2. Check whether the evidence is complete and reliable
  3. Validate applicable control, trade or compliance requirements
  4. Request further documents or business context where needed
  5. Determine whether the issue is valid, explainable or requires escalation
  6. Record the authorised disposition and follow-up action
Control principle: AI supports prioritisation and investigation; qualified people retain interpretation, approval and final decision authority.
What does not change: accountable auditors and specialists still own interpretation, legal or policy conclusions, finding acceptance, remediation and consequential decisions. The agent accelerates analysis preparation; it does not replace professional judgement.
03 / GOVERNANCE & ACCOUNTABILITY

Potential issues are signals for review, not final findings. Compliance specialists and authorised business owners validate evidence, apply the relevant framework and decide the disposition.

EXPORT / TRADE-COMPLIANCE ROUTE

Agent flags incomplete classification, authorisation, end-user / end-use or party-screening evidence → review queue assigns the item to a trade-compliance specialist → specialist checks current law, official sources and transaction facts → authorised human records the disposition. The agent never declares a shipment legally cleared.

SPECIALIST VERIFICATION REQUIRED
Decision contract: tool result + source record IDs + test parameters + evidence gaps + alternative explanations + reviewer identity + human decision. The demo captures a draft review state in the browser session; it does not persist an enterprise audit log.
