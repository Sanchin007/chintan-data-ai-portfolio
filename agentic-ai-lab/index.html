01 / CONTINUOUS AUDITING

From one-off checks to a managed control cycle

Risk-based schedules and event triggers feed repeatable, versioned control runs. Stable finding keys prevent duplicate cases across reruns; ageing, ownership, evidence requests, escalation and retesting create a complete lifecycle.

  • Daily / weekly / period-close control packs
  • Source watermark and data-quality gates
  • Exception deduplication and SLA ageing
  • Human disposition, remediation and retest
02 / DATA FOUNDATION

Canonical audit data model

Separate control, source, run, finding, evidence, review, remediation and benefits entities. Preserve source lineage, control version, calculation, snapshot and append-only reviewer events.

  • BigQuery Standard SQL reference DDL
  • Star-schema facts and dimensions
  • Stable idempotency and current-state views
  • Coverage, freshness and ageing measures
03 / GOVERNED ANALYTICS

Decision-ready data products

Curated control-run and finding facts power executive assurance, domain analytics, review queues and benefits reporting. Measures are explicitly defined and tied to a source and time period.

  • Control execution and population coverage
  • Open, overdue and escalated findings
  • Evidence completeness and review cycle time
  • Recurrence and remediation effectiveness

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED

Evidence collection

Product description and technical specification, origin, destination, exporter, consignee, end-user, end-use, classification rationale, screening evidence and authorisation or no-authorisation rationale.

Specialist verification

Confirm jurisdictional nexus, current control-list version, classification, official-party-screening result, licence conditions, diversion risk and transaction facts.

Controlled disposition

Record evidence, reviewer identity, legal basis, timestamp, decision conditions and escalation. No AI-generated shipment clearance or legal conclusion.
