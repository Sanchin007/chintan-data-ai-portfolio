PROCESS MAP · AS-IS CONCEPT VS PROPOSED TO-BE

From fragmented coordination to evidence-led orchestration.

A representative enterprise process model across engineering context, maritime and air-freight operations. The baseline is illustrative and should not be read as a verified map of a specific CMA CGM or Dassault Systèmes internal procedure.

Before · Traditional coordination

Typical fragmented workflow to be assessed and validated with process owners.

01 · SOURCE

Separate operational inputs

Engineering or virtual-twin outputs, maritime movements, air-cargo records, finance and supplier data remain in their respective systems.

02 · CONSOLIDATE

Manual data collection

Teams extract spreadsheets, reports and status updates across functions and transport modes.

03 · RECONCILE

Human-led comparison

Analysts compare milestones, costs, operational records and supporting evidence using separate checks.

04 · ESCALATE

Email and meeting follow-up

Exceptions, missing documents and ownership questions are routed between teams for clarification.

05 · REPORT

Manual evidence pack

Findings and decision material are assembled for management or audit review.

After · Proposed AI-assisted workflow

Target-state design demonstrated with synthetic data and bounded tools.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Natural-language mission intake

Capture the business question, scope, entities, period, transport mode and review constraints.

02 · ORCHESTRATE

Gemini selects specialist checks

Route the request to allow-listed finance, pricing, procurement, supplier and fleet tools.

03 · VALIDATE

Deterministic evidence checks

Reconcile synthetic records, calculate variances and identify missing or inconsistent evidence.

04 · CHALLENGE

Trace findings and limitations

Expose source references, assumptions, alternative explanations and evidence gaps.

05 · APPROVE

Human-reviewed workpaper

Prepare a draft report for accountable reviewers; no autonomous operational or compliance decisions.
