05 / Enterprise operating model

From one-off analytics to continuous assurance

A controlled operating model for repeatable audit coverage, exception triage and accountable decisions.

TARGET OPERATING MODEL
01 · INGEST & GOVERN

Trusted data foundation

ERP finance, procurement, supplier master, pricing, fleet and logistics data enter through approved, read-only interfaces.

  • Source owner and refresh timestamp
  • Schema and completeness checks
  • Lineage, access policy and retention
02 · ANALYSE

Repeatable control tests

SQL and Python analytics calculate defined control indicators with versioned rules and reproducible parameters.

  • Duplicate and three-way match tests
  • Threshold clustering and change-to-pay
  • Pricing, margin and utilisation outliers
03 · ORCHESTRATE

Gemini decision support

The orchestrator interprets the audit objective, selects allow-listed tools and explains results with explicit evidence boundaries.

  • Bounded tool calls and structured outputs
  • Evidence challenge and counter-hypotheses
  • No autonomous remediation or approval
04 · ASSURE

Human-led audit workflow

Auditors validate evidence, resolve context, approve conclusions and assign management actions.

  • Reviewer sign-off and segregation of duties
  • Finding owner, due date and status
  • Immutable event history in target design

GOVERNANCE & RISK CONTROLS

Guardrails before enterprise deployment

Data protectionPurpose limitation, minimisation, residency assessment, retention schedule and role-based access.
Model riskApproved model inventory, prompt/version control, evaluation set, drift review and fallback path.
AuditabilityInput snapshot IDs, test version, parameters, tool trace, evidence references and reviewer actions.
SecuritySecrets in managed vault, authenticated API, least privilege, rate limits and security monitoring.
Human accountabilityNo automated adverse decisions, legal conclusions, payment holds or remediation writes.
Operational resilienceFailure queues, retries, idempotency, reconciliation and documented incident ownership.
