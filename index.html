      SOLUTION DEVELOPMENT MODEL

      A repeatable path from unstable requirements to production-ready delivery.

      The sequence matters: understand the real need, make requirements explicit, compare solution options, prototype uncertainty, hand over without losing intent, and learn from production evidence.

      01
      UNDERSTAND

      Clarify intent & constraints

      Identify users, business outcome, customer constraints, existing systems, unresolved questions and success measures.

      02
      SPECIFY

      Make requirements buildable

      Define functional behaviour, acceptance criteria, edge cases, data needs and non-functional expectations before engineering starts.

      03
      DESIGN

      Compare viable options

      Model integrations, APIs, data flows and dependencies; make trade-offs across reliability, performance, security, cost and reuse explicit.

      04
      PROTOTYPE

      De-risk uncertainty

      Build focused PoCs for the assumptions that matter: model behaviour, API feasibility, latency, data contracts or integration boundaries.

      05
      HANDOVER

      Protect solution intent

      Give delivery squads clear artifacts, remain available for clarification and validate that the implementation still solves the original problem.

      06
      OBSERVE

      Learn from production

      Track reliability, quality, security, support themes, adoption and business outcome; feed evidence into the next design iteration.
