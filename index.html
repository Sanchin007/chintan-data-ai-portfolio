      DELIVERY MODEL

      A repeatable data-to-decision operating model for production analytics and ML.

      The sequence matters. I start with the business decision, engineer trustworthy data, validate the analytical method, define controls and keep monitoring after deployment so the product can learn from real outcomes.

      01
      FRAME

      Define the decision

      Clarify the business question, action owner, success measure and constraints before selecting data or algorithms.

      02
      ENGINEER

      Build the data product

      Ingest, transform and model the required sources into reliable analytical datasets and reusable features.

      03
      GOVERN

      Control quality & lineage

      Apply validation, reconciliation, ownership, access, lineage and audit controls before downstream consumption.

      04
      MODEL

      Analyse / predict

      Use statistical analysis, forecasting or ML appropriate to the problem, then evaluate against a meaningful baseline.

      05
      DECIDE

      Explain & act

      Translate outputs into drivers, thresholds, recommendations and a controlled business action or human-review path.

      06
      MONITOR

      Measure & learn

      Track pipeline health, data quality, model behaviour and business outcome; feed production evidence into the next iteration.
